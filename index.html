Reading HackRadar responsibly

Network observations are suspicious-source IPs from SANS Internet Storm Center / DShield. They are not confirmed breaches. CISA advisories are known exploited vulnerabilities from CISA KEV, not individual incidents. Their catalog addition dates are not attack timestamps.

Regional radars filter geographically located source IPs in the limited global feed, not verified attacks against those countries. An empty regional radar is not evidence of safety. Banking includes documented bank/payment-system cyber incidents, separately from verified service outages. A service outage is not evidence of a cyberattack. Financial incident records are individually sourced and manually curated, not an exhaustive or instant threat feed. Sector filters only show incidents explicitly classified by trusted sources; we will not guess targets from IPs, countries, or product names. An empty sector does not mean that sector is safe. Historical trends are counts of records visible in our snapshots, not global attack volume. No data is invented to fill gaps.
