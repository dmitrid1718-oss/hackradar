What you are seeing

Network observations: IP addresses and reports collected by SANS Internet Storm Center from participating sensors. A source IP can be a proxy, VPN, cloud server or compromised machine. Geolocation is approximate and may be wrong. Reporting does not prove an attack was successful.

Advisories: Entries from CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog. Dates indicate when they entered the catalog, not when an attack happened. Advisories are global, so they are not mapped to invented coordinates.

Sources & reliability

SANS Internet Storm Center / DShield — network source observations. Attribution: SANS Technology Institute, Internet Storm Center.

CISA KEV — confirmed exploited vulnerabilities. Geolocation supplied by ipwho.is. Feed updates are automated, not real-time.
