Silence is not zero.

When an expected report stops arriving, the instrument should say so. Missing data is not evidence that incidents stopped—or that anyone deliberately silenced a feed.

What the labels mean

Current: published report is within the expected window. Stale: report is older than its expected window. Unavailable: file cannot be fetched or parsed. Invalid: timestamp is missing, malformed or implausibly in the future. These are data-health signals, not threat assessments.

Expected windows below are provisional editorial thresholds, not proof of a publisher's schedule. Source-specific schedules and collector logs should be reviewed before automated notifications are enabled.
