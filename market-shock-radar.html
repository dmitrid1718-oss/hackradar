MARKET MOMENTUM / SUDDEN MOVES

When the market jumps, what changed?

Track abrupt moves across markets, separate observed prices from possible explanations, and look for cross-market confirmation. This is an investigation board—not a trading signal.

Proposed event triggers

  • Single stock: 5% or larger daily move; distinguish earnings/news from unexplained movement.
  • Major index: 2% or larger daily move or an unusually sharp intraday reversal.
  • 10-year Treasury yield: 15 basis points or larger daily move.
  • Volatility: significant VIX jump, evaluated against its recent baseline.

These are initial editorial thresholds, not calibrated anomaly detection. Historical comparisons and market-session timestamps are required before labeling a move unusual.

What each alert should contain

DATE / TIME · INSTRUMENT · % OR BASIS POINTS

What changed: Verified price movement and the relevant market session.

Status: Confirmed price / reported catalyst / unknown cause.

Why watch: Whether peers, indexes, yields or volatility corroborate the move.

Source: Direct link and data timestamp. If unavailable, withhold the number.
