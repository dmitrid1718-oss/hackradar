How we would detect a real increase

Use a stable source set and exposure denominator across comparable 30- and 90-day periods. Deduplicate repeated coverage of one incident, distinguish date of incident from date of article, and record the affected sector, operational impact, official explanation, and confirmation status. Separate data-feed outages from true zeroes. Only assess apparent clusters after a baseline exists; correlation is not attribution.

Priority sectors: aviation and transport communications, power grid, water and wastewater, industrial controls and refrigeration, telecom, healthcare, food and logistics. We will not label normal faults as cyberattacks.
