What would count as evidence?

  • Primary incident reports with matching timelines and technical causes.
  • Independent corroboration beyond recycled headlines or social-media posts.
  • Shared dependencies or forensic indicators—not merely similar dates.
  • Counterexamples and a historical baseline showing whether the cluster is unusual.

Working rules

Keep incident date separate from publication date. Label each observation confirmed, reported, contested or unresolved. Record corrections openly. If evidence fails to support a link, say so. An ordinary explanation is a finding, too.
